A block world where everything obeys physics. Wood burns, TNT levels buildings, and unsupported structures fall apart.

WASD move, Shift run, Space jump or swim. Mouse looks (arrow keys too). Left click breaks (hold), right click places. Slots 1–4 are Pick, Fire, Sea and TNT (left and right click do different things) and 5–9 hold blocks. E opens the block, world and disaster menu. N triggers a random disaster. Fire: left flamethrower, right water hose.
Touch: left stick moves, drag right side to look, buttons act.
